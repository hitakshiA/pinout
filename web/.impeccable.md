# Pinout

## Design Context

### Users

Two audiences, in this order of priority:

1. **Hedera x402 bounty judges.** They know the x402 standard cold. Explaining what x402 is insults
   them. What they need is why this belongs on Hedera specifically, and evidence it actually runs.
   They will click through to HashScan and to the repo. Anything unverifiable is a liability.
2. **Developers building agents.** They want to know what they can rent, what it costs, and how to
   point their own agent at it. They arrive skeptical of crypto-adjacent infrastructure claims.

The job to be done: convince a technical reader in under two minutes that an AI agent can rent real
GPUs by the second, pay for them itself over x402, and that the bill is provable rather than
asserted.

### Brand Personality

**Measured, exact, unbluffing.**

Pinout's differentiator is honesty under scrutiny: every number on the site is measured, the
verifier is built to catch the seller, and the known limits are published rather than hidden. The
interface has to feel like it was built by someone who checks their own work. Confidence comes from
specificity, never from adjectives.

Emotional goal: *this is real, and I can verify it myself.*

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme: dark.** Derived from context, not preference. The audience is developers and judges reading
technical infrastructure documentation, and every product in this category (RunPod, Modal, Hedera's
own developer surfaces) is dark. Light mode is supported via a token-level override but dark is the
designed default.

**References (explicit, from the client):** runpod.io/product/cloud-gpus and modal.com, cloned
structurally with their real assets. This is a deliberate constraint: as a bounty entry we cannot
out-design an established GPU cloud, so we borrow their credibility patterns and brand the result
for Hedera. RunPod's ground (#06040D), glass surfaces, pill radii, ABC Diatype and hero artwork are
in use. The accent is Hedera purple (#8259EF), which occupies the slot RunPod's #5D29F0 held.

**Anti-references (rejected by the client during this project):**
- A "component datasheet" treatment. Too quiet, and the audience does not read it as credible.
- Any layout that reads as a generic AI landing page.
- Competitor logos or customer walls we cannot truthfully claim.

### Design Principles

1. **Every number is measured.** No illustrative figures, no rounded-for-looks values. If a number
   appears on screen it came from a real run and can be checked against the mirror node.
2. **Hedera is load bearing, not a badge.** The four platform properties that make this design
   possible get argued with evidence, not listed as logos in a trust strip.
3. **Show the mechanism, don't describe it.** The meter, the pause, the refund are the product.
   Demonstrate them in the interface rather than writing paragraphs about them.
4. **Publish the limits.** Unit economics, cold starts and unverified figures are stated openly.
   Concealment is the one thing that would destroy the credibility the rest of the page builds.
5. **Borrowed structure, owned substance.** The layout is cloned; the content, evidence and voice
   must be unmistakably ours.
